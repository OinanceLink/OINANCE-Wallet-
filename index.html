STEP 1 OF 4

Create your wallet

A real non-custodial wallet needs to generate cryptographic keys securely on your device. This website prototype does not yet do that.

  • Generate a secure wallet key
  • Back up a recovery phrase safely
  • Set up wallet access protection
  • Verify recovery before using funds
The next screens are previews only. They will not generate a recovery phrase or create an actual wallet.

STEP 2 OF 4

Back up your recovery phrase

A genuine wallet recovery phrase can restore access to your funds. Anyone who obtains it may be able to control those funds.

  • Only use a phrase generated by a properly implemented wallet.
  • Write it down privately and store it securely offline.
  • Never share it with anyone, including support staff.
  • Never enter it into this demonstration website.
No recovery words are displayed here because this prototype cannot generate a real, secure recovery phrase.

STEP 3 OF 4

Preview PIN setup

A finished wallet may use a PIN to unlock the app. A PIN alone is not enough to protect wallet funds.

This preview PIN is not saved or used to protect anything.
